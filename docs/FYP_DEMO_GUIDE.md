# Simple operating guide

This guide explains the two presenter roles without publishing local accounts, customer identifiers, exact case links or private agreement details. Keep a detailed private runbook separately.

## Open the application

1. Run **Install Hermes Agent.bat** once, then **Run BeLive Prototype.bat**.
2. Open [the local staff dashboard](http://127.0.0.1:8877/) and use an account for the selected database. First launch creates a new administrator; there is no common password.
3. Open **System setup → AI & knowledge → Connection settings → Test AI connection**. Wait for the actual passed/failed result.
4. Check the executor and model separately: official native Hermes runs the workflow, GPT-6 Luna supplies the model. Internet access and provider credits are needed.
5. Obtain the approved knowledge archive and matching recovery key privately. Copy the archive to `knowledge/team-memory.enc.json` before importing the key in **Self-learning → Team knowledge backup → Restore on another computer**. Code download does not include knowledge. Before updating an older installation, export/preserve both files outside the checkout and retain your own `.env`, data/database and credential key. Follow [private knowledge transfer](SHARED_MEMORY.md), [AI setup](AI_KEY_SETUP.md) and [connection settings](CREDENTIAL_SETTINGS.md).

## Know your role

| Presenter | Start | Show |
|---|---|---|
| Public-monitoring presenter | Public reviews | A permitted source, collection route, complaint decision, level/reason, staff destination and tailored preview. |
| Support presenter | WhatsApp support | The private conversation, ticket, approved knowledge, AI answer or staff handover and reviewed lesson reuse. |

The website is staff operations/contact entry. Once the authorised real integration is verified, customers receive support in the same WhatsApp chat. Until then, clearly label a simulator demonstration as local QA; do not claim it reached a real customer.

## Support presenter: try a conversation

1. Open **WhatsApp inbox → Test a conversation safely — never sends to WhatsApp**.
2. Use a new fictional customer reference for each independent scenario. The same reference continues the same conversation.
3. Try a routine house-rule enquiry, a shared-appliance fault and a prolonged access/security concern. These are generic topics, not company policy statements.
4. Wait for the actual result. Read Complaint/Enquiry, category, SIMPLE/MODERATE/HIGH, **Why this level** and **Staff destination**.
5. Expand **AI activity and sources**. Inspect actual native execution and current approved citations. A configuration indicator alone does not show the Agent answered.
6. For MODERATE/HIGH, distinguish the acknowledgement shown to the customer from **AI assistance · staff review required** and its **Draft evidence**.

On a prepared installation, select the labelled `friday-` rehearsal conversations by topic. Local identifiers and account details differ between databases; they are not shared through source code.

| Level | Expected rule |
|---|---|
| Supported SIMPLE | A checked informational answer can be automatic. |
| MODERATE | AI assists; substantive content stays with staff for review and operational decisions. |
| HIGH or immediate danger | Staff takes priority; danger bypasses routine troubleshooting. |
| Uncertain, unsupported or failed | Request staff guidance without inventing facts. |

Use the actual visible result rather than promising a label. A physical repair still needs a person.

## Staff supplies the solution, Hermes writes the reply

1. Open a conversation waiting for staff and read **AI asks staff**.
2. Under **Give AI a verified solution**, enter checked guidance. Operational arrangements and outcomes in a rehearsal must be explicitly fictional.
3. Confirm the verification box and select **Ask AI to reply**.
4. Inspect the actual native reply, its sources and the linked ticket. The internal note stays private.
5. Staff completes any real action, records evidence and verifies the outcome before closure.

An AI acknowledgement or work-order queue entry does not prove a person was contacted or work completed.

## Explain self-learning

Say: “After a case is solved and checked, staff can approve a useful general lesson. A later similar request retrieves that lesson before replying.”

1. Open a verified completed test case. Show its actual reply and recorded test outcome.
2. Under **Teach future replies**, approve general wording or save a reviewed correction. Remove personal information and avoid turning past operational facts into permanent policy.
3. Open **Self-learning → Reviewed lessons from completed cases** or the approved shared-experience list.
4. Submit a related new question. Inspect the exact matched lesson and citation in the new reply's evidence.
5. Use **Test approved complaint experience** for the same question without and with the approved lesson. Compare supported facts, useful steps, relevance and repetition.

The completed-case rehearsal has genuine native replies and later lesson reuse, with scripted fictional staff review/outcomes and SAMPLE / TEST labels. Both baseline and lesson-assisted answers can be useful; do not invent an improvement percentage. **Legacy demo writing tips** measure formatting only and are not the main proof of support quality.

Keep detailed per-case proof private. The model is unchanged: this is retrieval-based learning, not weight retraining or verified long-term customer satisfaction.
