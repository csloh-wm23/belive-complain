# Staff help for WhatsApp

Open **Staff help** in the left menu. Each card is one issue, with the original customer message, Hermes's specific question and the relevant rule/research checks. A missing company decision is not something Hermes should guess from the Internet. Safe general questions can still be answered without staff.

1. In **Waiting for your guidance**, choose the issue and read its original message. Older records without a message binding require you to select the original message first.
2. Write the verified solution or next step. Include conditions and confirmed arrangements. You can tell Hermes to ask for one missing detail before continuing; do not invent an approval or completed repair.
3. Confirm the instructions and select **Give Hermes these next steps**. This saves internal guidance, not a direct customer message. The official native Hermes Agent retrieves it, adapts it to the customer's language and context, and passes an independent reply review.
4. **In progress · next steps given** shows the continuing issue. Guidance does not mean the issue is solved. Use **View conversation** to see the customer-facing reply and later answers.

Each issue keeps its own original-message binding and guidance revision. New customer questions take priority over queued staff guidance, while the queued guidance remains available. When Hermes returns to that issue after staff instructions or new evidence, the sender uses WhatsApp's actual quoted-reply `context.message_id`; a paragraph saying “about your earlier question” is not a substitute. The quote is looked up from the same customer's same case, never supplied as an arbitrary external ID. See [Meta's reply-to text format](https://www.postman.com/meta/whatsapp-business-platform/request/73yi2uj/send-reply-to-text-message).

Legacy staff tasks without a verified original-message binding remain visible for staff to bind manually, but are excluded from the Agent's resumable issue IDs and guidance evidence. They must not absorb a new question, block its reviewed independent reply or be marked answered just because the new question received help. The independent reply quotes its own current customer message.

Unresolved staff issues retain their urgency and staff queue even when a different, safe question receives an answer. Separate issues do not overwrite one another. If the customer sends a new message while Hermes is drafting, stale drafts are suppressed and staff guidance is queued for fresh generation/review. Closed cases, changed guidance, revoked knowledge, uncertain sends and the customer-service window still have sending checks. If the window expires before staff guidance can be sent, a new customer message can reopen it and queue that guidance for a fresh quoted reply. UNKNOWN sends are not automatically retried.

Hermes supports Malay, English, Chinese and mixed-language conversation. Small lexical hints assist common abbreviations, while the original wording remains intact. Examples include `sy` → `saya`, `xde` → `tak ada`, `xpe` → `tak apa`, and `u` → `you`. Context decides ambiguous forms such as `ur`; unclear meanings should prompt a clarification. “xpe” or “thanks” alone is not evidence of a repaired fault. Staff can give guidance in English while Hermes answers the customer's original Malay or Chinese message.

Individual instructions are scoped to that customer's issue. They do not automatically become public SOP or reusable self-learning knowledge. Existing reviewed-learning approval remains separate.

When a customer answers a verified staff collection step, a reviewed reply may continue that same ANSWERED issue using its cited instructions, even while the underlying fault remains uncertain. This does not reopen the task by itself. The continuation requires matching current guidance, stays limited to low/medium priority, and preserves explicit handover, new-risk, conflicting-SOP and independent-review checks. Guidance from other or closed issues cannot authorise it.

Replies use the customer's current language, register and emotional context rather than the wording of internal staff notes. Casual wording can receive a relaxed response, formal wording a professional response, and frustration a brief specific acknowledgement followed by a useful next step. There is no canned “bro” rewrite and no extra tone-classifier model call. The same native reply generation also produces a bounded `handover_reply` when staff is needed. The policy selects this acknowledgement before the independent review, which checks the exact public text and the separate private draft. Unsupported approvals, advice and promises are still blocked. Deterministic emergency and unavailable-model fallbacks remain available; normal handovers no longer always replace the generated wording with one form letter.

Verification: `test_whatsapp_issues.py` exercises issue isolation, pending-message ordering, quoted payloads, privacy, expiry and ambiguous acknowledgements. `browser_staff_help.py` runs a separate headless browser against a temporary database and simulated sends. `rehearse_whatsapp_staff.py` explicitly invokes genuine native Hermes/OpenAI on fictional multilingual scenarios and stores private execution evidence; it never sends to Meta. Run it manually when authorised to use the configured model.

Hermes separates issue urgency from the next support action: ANSWER, CLARIFY, RESEARCH or TEAM. A medium-priority fault may receive a reviewed clarification or evidence-backed non-invasive guidance without automatically creating a staff request. High-risk cases, explicit staff requests, private approvals and physical work still use the team path. The activity panel records the chosen action and a short factual reason; it does not expose hidden reasoning.

Staff help cards show the assigned queue and notification status. Maintenance requests go directly to the configured MAINTENANCE queue, even when the complaint category is ACCESS or WIFI, without an extra generic staff alert. PENDING_APPROVAL means an internal queued notice, not confirmed external delivery. Configure real team destinations and the notification service before claiming that a team has received the request.

`rehearse_autonomous_support.py` uses genuine native Hermes, the current eligible approved knowledge and actual official-source web search on fictional questions without routing hints. It saves private observed decisions separately from structural mocked regression tests and never sends simulated replies to Meta.

The native assessment can request an official public lookup before drafting; the reply agent can also request one during correction if no lookup actually occurred. A pre-research uncertainty flag is retained for the unresolved fault, while a bounded next step backed by fresh official evidence can receive its own independent safety review. For a matching already-open team issue, reviewed public technical help can be sent without closing that issue or duplicating the maintenance notice. This does not authorise new policy exceptions, physical repairs or claims of delivery/completed work.

Staff help now shows maintenance severity and numeric priority (1 information, 2 routine, 3 urgent, 4 emergency), the supplied property/room and intake readiness. For a confirmed physical defect, Hermes gathers only missing essential location and then submits the request directly to the configured maintenance queue. Optional access-time questions are not a prerequisite unless an actual team procedure requires them. Missing routine-job location leaves its notice in NEEDS_DETAILS; ready requests go to PENDING_APPROVAL, and neither status is proof of dispatch or team acceptance. Updated intake cancels the superseded unsent notice, keeping one active notice.

Use Finish this issue with checked evidence for a real resolution, or Test completed for a fictional rehearsal. The latter does not claim a real repair or create an approved lesson. Finished issues appear in Finished issues; the chat and other topics remain open. Unconfirmed sends block completion, and stale queued guidance for the finished issue is cancelled. A new customer report can reopen that same issue.

Unknown-brand appliance photos can trigger an IDENTIFICATION lookup using visible public labels and product-series clues before an exact model or OCR code is confirmed. Candidate product families do not establish the customer's device or a fault diagnosis. Typed display codes and follow-ups remain bound to the selected photo, excluding unrelated earlier appliances. The reply and independent reviewer receive details the customer cannot supply, so an unavailable label or clearer photo should not be requested repeatedly. If official evidence still cannot support safe help, staff receive the findings, source links and remaining uncertainty. Follow-ups for the same open photo issue update that request instead of creating another one.

Photo-specific official findings remain available for one hour within that customer's case; an identical query can reuse them, while a refined query can search again. Expired evidence or changed domain restrictions require a fresh lookup. These temporary findings are neither approved SOP nor public self-learning lessons. `rehearse_photo_context.py` exercises the genuine native agents and real official-source lookup with simulated WhatsApp transport; the live chat must be checked separately before claiming delivery.
