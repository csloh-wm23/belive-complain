# Company research: BeLive Ventures / BeLive CoLiving

The user explicitly confirmed the Malaysian BeLive Ventures / CoLiving company. It is not BeLive Technology's live-streaming software business. Research informed the tenant-support categories; it does not establish a tenancy contract or authorise access to private systems.

| Primary source | Relevant information | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| [Official BeLive website](https://www.belive.asia/) | Furnished co-living rooms; Wi-Fi, cleaning, smart access and maintenance described publicly | Support categories include Wi-Fi, cleaning, access and maintenance |
| [About BeLive](https://www.belive.asia/about) | Rental/co-living business and operational focus, including renovation and technology | Tenant support should use property/condo context and route work to staff |
| [Official rental-readiness article](https://www.belive.asia/our-blog/a-practical-guide-on-rental-room-renovation-tips-co-living-design-types-and-how-to-renovate-rental-rooms-for-rental-readiness) | Describes the property journey and references RenoXpert and Spacify | Verify operational records with BeLive; do not invent a Spacify API |
| [BeLive application sign-in](https://app.belive.asia/sign-in) | Private application entry point | No automated login, credential handling or private-data extraction was performed |

Public marketing statements are not proof of a particular tenant's entitlements, repair outcome, price, refund, availability or response-time promise. The broader proposal also described move coordination, but the user's team has clarified that move-in/out arrangements, inspections and random PIC allocation are outside this module's responsibility.

The prototype's 30-minute acknowledgement, 24-hour follow-up, 15-minute source interval, and seven-day portal key are implementation defaults/assumptions. They are not claimed as existing BeLive policies.

Research was used to narrow the system to tenant operations. No move-in/out, inspection, PIC allocation, owner, marketing or sales workflow is enabled.
