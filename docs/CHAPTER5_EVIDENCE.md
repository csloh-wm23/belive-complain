# Results and methodology checklist

Use this public checklist to organise a results chapter in the existing report format. It is not a completed report or a claim of submission. Personal names, meeting details, contact numbers, customer content, exact record identifiers and private source documents belong in restricted evidence, not a public repository.

## Scope and expected outcomes

The public-monitoring role collects selected permitted public complaints, determines relevance/severity and prepares a routed reply preview. The support role handles private tenant conversations, approved knowledge, staff-guidance relay and checked outcomes. Both share tickets, distinct native agent roles and reviewed lesson reuse. The primary private customer channel is WhatsApp; the website is staff operations/contact entry. Marketing, sales, owner services and move-in/out are outside this scope.

## Functionality discussion

| Function | Evidence to inspect privately | Discussion |
|---|---|---|
| Collection | Collection route, immutable source/version, original link and duplicate result. | Distinguish browser/API/manual collection and bounded access. |
| Assessment and routing | Actual decision, evidence, category, level reason and configured destination. | A star rating or angry tone alone is not severity; uncertainty and operational actions need staff. |
| Native agents | Actual executor class/version, calls, role names and tools. | Hermes executes the agent loop; the GPT model is its provider. Distinct jobs do not require separate servers. |
| Approved knowledge | Relevant current citation and version. | Past replies and public technical sources cannot establish private company policy. |
| Staff-guidance relay | Internal verified instruction and resulting model reply. | Staff supplies operational facts and performs real actions. |
| Learning | Verified completed test case, reviewed general lesson and later matching citation. | Preserve fictional/sample provenance; rejection or reopening revokes reuse. |
| Photo and research | Visible observations/uncertainty, bounded search and allowed citations. | Do not infer a real fault from a synthetic image or claim a generic source establishes equipment identity. |
| Outcomes and alerts | Ownership, due work, checked closure and notification state. | Separate queued, accepted and delivered; customer claims are distinct from verified outcomes. |
| Credentials | Blank secret inputs, configured/source status and connection result. | Key presence is not a successful model check or platform permission. |

Model-call counts establish execution in the named test groups. They are not independent quality samples or percentages. The support phases total 35 calls; the nested search-provider request is separate. Detailed evidence stays private with provenance and test labels.

## Evaluation method

For each test record **input type, expected rule, actual result, evidence, pass/fail and limitation**. Compare the same question before/after a reviewed lesson, identify the added guidance and inspect a later matching citation. Discuss relevance, supported facts, useful next steps and repeated questions. A changed answer, citation count or word-count score does not prove improved support.

Customer anger may reflect unfinished service work, unclear wording or both. Review the actual linked response/follow-up before approving a lesson. Never convert an unverified past outcome into company policy. This is retrieval-based learning, not weight retraining.

Customer satisfaction, time saved, broad accuracy and long-term improvement with real tenants have not been established by these rehearsals. State those limits.

## Figures and deliverables

Prepare redacted figures showing two entry points and one queue, explained assessment/routing, native role/tool evidence, approved knowledge citations, staff relay, reviewed lesson reuse, photo uncertainty and outcome/alert states. Preserve the report's own numbering. Use generic labels in public copies; detailed identifiers and original customer material stay restricted.

Deliverables include a working local prototype, setup/operating instructions, bounded test scripts, a functionality/results discussion and a limitations table. Real WhatsApp delivery, scheduled company API reads and external staff notifications require their own authorised acceptance evidence. Public platform responses remain drafts/previews during testing.
