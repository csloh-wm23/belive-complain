| Role | Starting point | What to demonstrate |
|---|---|---|
| Public-monitoring presenter | A selected permitted public review | Collection route, source provenance, complaint decision, explained severity, staff routing and a tailored preview. |
| Support presenter | A private support conversation | Approved knowledge, native reply, staff handover, verified staff-guidance relay and outcome tracking. |

Use [the operating guide](FYP_DEMO_GUIDE.md) and [rehearsal checklist](FRIDAY_DEMO_CHECKLIST.md). Keep customer identifiers, exact case/lesson links, private source material and account details out of public slides and repositories.

## Suggested order

1. Show two entry points leading to one ticket queue. The website is staff operations/contact entry; connected private customer support stays in WhatsApp.
2. Public-monitoring presenter: import or open permitted review evidence, name the route used, show assessment/destination and a separate tailored preview. Clearly identify saved evidence if collection is unavailable.
3. Support presenter: if real delivery is verified, use an authorised same-chat test. Otherwise label the local simulator honestly. Select prepared rehearsal conversations by topic: routine information, shared-appliance fault and prolonged access/security concern.
4. Show the customer acknowledgement versus the held substantive staff draft. Staff supplies checked guidance, then Hermes adapts it into the customer reply.
5. Trace an approved general lesson from a verified closed test case to a later matching reply. Compare the same question without/with the lesson and discuss the actual difference.
6. Show photo uncertainty, bounded official-source research, outcomes and staff alerts.
7. Explain the external permissions and production limits still unresolved.

## What the measured results prove

The regression suite passed **297 tests**, zero failures/errors/skips. Headless workflow checks passed. Separate genuine native runs recorded 15 calls across three support-policy scenarios, 35 across learning/photo/research and four across public analysis/preview. These prove specific workflow executions, not general correctness or customer satisfaction.

The learning outcomes were scripted test activity with genuine model replies and explicit sample provenance. Public collection read visible selected low-rated content, not all reviews or every platform. Public responses remained previews and WhatsApp test output remained simulated.

## Keep external access separate

Official native Hermes readiness and model credits do not connect WhatsApp. Real support requires authorised business/number access, supported onboarding retaining the Business phone app, a reachable HTTPS webhook and observed reply/delivery receipts. Google Business Profile, Facebook and Threads need their own resources, permissions and live scans. Queued staff notifications need verified delivery.
